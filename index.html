About

background

I'm Kartik Nayak, a graduating high school senior from SIS Semarang in Indonesia and an incoming Electronic Engineering freshman at the University of Hong Kong with a 100% scholarship. Growing up around different cultures, languages, and learning systems shaped how I think about education, technology, and the kind of impact I want to make.
