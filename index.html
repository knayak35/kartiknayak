Things I’ve worked on

  • Global Science Engineering Organization
    Research

    I co-founded and helped lead a student-run STEM organization focused on making research and scientific communication more accessible to students. The platform publishes research articles, student interviews, and science-focused content, while also giving students a place to write, edit, review, and discuss technical ideas more seriously.

    My role involved research planning, editing articles, coordinating with team members, helping shape the publishing process, and thinking about how to turn student curiosity into something more structured. It taught me a lot about research communication, leadership, and how difficult but important it is to make technical ideas understandable.

    Research writing Editing STEM community
  • Star Generation
    Teaching

    I teach younger students in Math Olympiad, IGCSE, and A Level subjects. A lot of my teaching focuses on helping students stop seeing hard problems as random tricks and start seeing them as patterns, structures, and methods they can reuse.

    I especially enjoy explaining difficult ideas in a way that feels less intimidating. Teaching has also made me much better at communicating technical concepts, because it forces me to understand the idea deeply enough to explain it simply. This is one of the reasons becoming a professor is such an important long-term goal for me.

    Math Olympiad IGCSE A Level
  • SIS Alumni Platform
    Software

    I helped build a school alumni networking platform designed to connect current students with alumni through profiles, messaging, blog posts, and discovery features. The goal was to make university advice, mentorship, and alumni support easier to access inside the school community.

    This project gave me practical experience with building software around real users, not just features. I had to think about how students would actually search for alumni, what information would be useful, and how the platform could make conversations between students and graduates easier to start.

    Web development Community tools Mentorship
  • Numbify
    Education

    Numbify is an education project I built for younger math students. It was designed to make primary math more visual, structured, and easier to understand through lessons, worksheets, quizzes, and progress tracking.

    Instead of only giving students more practice questions, I wanted the platform to help them understand what they were doing and why. Building it made me think more carefully about educational design, student motivation, and how technology can make learning feel less confusing.

    EdTech Math learning Product design
  • ZNotes Education
    Content

    I contributed Cambridge-focused learning resources and notes for students preparing for exams. The work focused on turning syllabus content into explanations that are easier to revise from, easier to apply, and more aligned with what students actually need when preparing under time pressure.

    This experience strengthened my interest in educational writing and technical explanation. It also connected closely with the way I like to learn: reducing complicated topics into clear structures without removing the depth that makes them useful.

    Cambridge exams Notes writing Student resources
