01 / About me

I’m driven by the moment a difficult idea starts to make sense.

I grew up in Semarang, Indonesia, where Olympiad mathematics first taught me to enjoy sitting with a hard problem instead of rushing toward an answer. That habit now shapes how I approach computer science, probability, statistics, optimisation, and financial markets.

I learn best by moving between theory and practice: building a model, questioning its assumptions, then explaining it to someone else. Through research, teaching, publishing, and student leadership, I’ve learned that clear thinking and clear communication are inseparable. At HKU, I hope to deepen both.
